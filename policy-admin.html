Eligibility

Active full-time employees, 30+ hrs/week; 30-day new-hire waiting period

Classes

Executives (NWM-EXEC) and Salaried (NWM-SAL), 100% employer-paid. Hourly not covered at issue (drives the back-dated example)

Benefits

60% of earnings up to $10,000/mo; 180-day elimination; own-occ 24 mo; $30k survivor; 10% rehab incentive

Premium

$6,000/month at issue, quoted per $100 of covered monthly payroll; self-billed by employer

Open questions to validate

  • Can Implementation Managers generate standard contracts directly, with periodic audits by senior Contract Analysts?
  • What are the formal handover criteria between Implementation and the Contract team, and between Underwriting and Implementation?
  • What fiduciary or recordkeeping liability does Aflac take on if employer data is held in the portal (Legal review)?
